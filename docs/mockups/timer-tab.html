Why B

  • Same skeleton as Music (tile · title · subtitle · bar · prev/play/next), so the two tabs feel like one product.
  • The ring tile carries the timer's orange the way the cover carries the song's colour.
  • "Ends at 14:35" answers the question people actually have while a timer runs.
  • +1 min fills the "next" slot with something useful; drop it if you'd rather not add a feature (the row then becomes Reset/Cancel · circle).
